Kethan Artzz — Visual Storyteller for the Big Screen

IMAGINE · CREATE · EVOLVE

I'm a graphic designer based in India, specializing in movie poster and publicity campaign design. My work has shaped the visual identity for major theatrical releases — from Salaar and Devara to WAR 2, Kuravaali, and campaigns spanning both Indian and Hollywood cinema. Since 2019, I've been crafting key art that doesn't just advertise a film — it captures its emotional core in a single frame. Every poster is a puzzle: balancing star power, mood, typography, and story into something that stops people mid-scroll. Available for freelance and full-time collaborations — always looking for the next story worth telling visually.
